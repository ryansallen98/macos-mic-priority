# Privacy

Mic Priority is intentionally local and minimal.

## Data the app accesses

- Names and stable identifiers of audio input devices reported by Core Audio
- Whether each microphone is currently connected
- Which microphone is the current macOS default input
- The priority order and enabled state you configure

## Data the app does not access

- Microphone audio
- Recordings or transcripts
- Contacts, files, location, Bluetooth pairing data, or accounts

## Storage and networking

Preferences remain on your Mac in UserDefaults. The app contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, update checker, or other network functionality. No data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.
